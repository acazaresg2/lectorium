Chapter 1: The Discovery / Capítulo 1: El descubrimiento

It was a rainy Tuesday afternoon when Oliver found an old wooden box in the attic. The box was covered in dust, and its lock was rusty.

Era una tarde lluviosa de martes cuando Oliver encontró una vieja caja de madera en el ático.

"Emma, come here! You have to see this!" he shouted.

"¡Emma, ven aquí! ¡Tienes que ver esto!", gritó Oliver.

Emma arrived carrying a small notebook. She looked at the box with curiosity.

"Some doors are meant to stay closed." / "Algunas puertas están hechas para permanecer cerradas."

The lock was stubborn, but after a few attempts it finally clicked. Inside they found a folded note and a tiny silver key.

Note / Nota: The inscription contained á, é, í, ó, ú, ñ and ordinary English characters.

  • old-fashioned
  • blue-green
  • half-open
  • mysterious
