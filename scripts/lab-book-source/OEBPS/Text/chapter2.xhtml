Chapter 2: The Search / Capítulo 2: La búsqueda

The note contained a simple sentence: “Find the room beneath the old clock.”

La nota contenía una frase sencilla: “Busca la habitación debajo del reloj antiguo.”

Oliver and Emma searched the house from top to bottom. They checked the library, the kitchen, and even the garden.

"This is taking forever," Emma said. "Maybe we're looking in the wrong place."

  1. Check the attic.
  2. Check the library.
  3. Check the hallway.
  4. Check the room beneath the clock.

At the end of the hallway they noticed a narrow, almost invisible door.

The key did not fit. They tried again — slowly, carefully, patiently.

"Wait," said Emma. "There is another keyhole."

"Espera", dijo Emma. "Hay otra cerradura."

Behind the second panel they discovered a small passage leading downstairs.

The air smelled of stone, dust, and something strangely sweet.
