Chapter 3: The Reunion / Capítulo 3: El reencuentro

At the bottom of the stairs they found a circular room. In the center stood a stone pedestal.

On top of it lay a beautiful golden key, glowing softly in the darkness.

Oliver picked it up carefully. "This must be the key from the story."

Oliver la levantó con cuidado. "Esta debe ser la llave de la historia."

A hidden mechanism began to move. Somewhere above them, an old door opened.

They returned upstairs and found the wooden box waiting beside the grandfather clock.

The golden key opened a second compartment. Inside was a letter from their grandfather.

If you found this key, you were curious enough to search, patient enough to continue, and brave enough to discover.

Si encontraste esta llave, fuiste lo bastante curioso para buscar, paciente para continuar y valiente para descubrir.

The siblings smiled. The mystery was over — but another adventure was beginning.
